How to read this. The bar and line show how synthetic the voice sounds. When you speak, it should sit in the green “real voice” band. Play an AI-generated or cloned-voice clip through your speakers and it climbs toward red.

Read the score as low / medium / high suspicion, not as an exact probability — it is a relative measure of how synthetic a voice sounds.

It is most reliable on voice-synthesis methods it has seen before. Brand-new deepfakes are harder, and improving that is what this project is working on. It needs about 4 seconds of audio before the first solid reading.
